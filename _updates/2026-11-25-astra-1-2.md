project: astra
date: 2026-11-25 20:00:00 +0000

Astra's background is now alive, and it takes on the colors of the song that's playing. There are six themes: Cosmos, Classic, Retro, Futuristic, Koi pond and Outside, which shows the real weather where you are, right down to snow piling up on the cards. Playlists can have their own picture and color, and the phone can import .txt and .m3u playlists. On the phone, the next 15 minutes of music load ahead for the drive, and Auto quality keeps music playing on a weak signal. On the computer, Astra has its own window and a tidier row of Now Playing buttons. Long names slide across instead of being cut off, plus a round of fixes.
