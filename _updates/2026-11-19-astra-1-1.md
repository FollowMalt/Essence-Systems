---
title: Astra 1.1 is out
project: astra
date: 2026-11-19 21:00:00 +0000
---
Astra 1.1 is here, the same day as 1.0 reached the website. It adds Astra Radio, which turns every playlist into a station on the FM dial that plays all day, so you tune in mid-song like a real radio. There's a new 10-band EQ with presets, and streaming from your computer to your phone is about 50% faster. Walkman pages are redesigned with smaller cards and a page of their own for each player, and we fixed visual bugs like album pages that looked off. Astra 1.1 is also ready for the Essence Launcher.
