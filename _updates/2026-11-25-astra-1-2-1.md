---
title: Astra 1.2.1, a reliability patch
project: astra
date: 2026-11-25 21:00:00 +0000
---
Updates now install reliably. Astra's built-in updater could sometimes fail to start the step that swaps in the new version, which left Astra closed with nothing happening, and that's fixed from 1.2.1 on. Song and artist names also no longer get squished on the phone when the lyrics are showing. We recommend 1.2.1 for everyone. Once the Essence Launcher is out, anyone on 1.0 or 1.1 can update through it. It takes over an existing Astra without reinstalling or touching your library.
