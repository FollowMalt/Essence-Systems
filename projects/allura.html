Essence · Allura · In development

Allura

A private AI assistant that lives on your own computer.

Allura runs locally, on hardware you already own, including older and low-end PCs. Your conversations stay on your machine instead of being sent to someone else's servers.

Why we're building her

A good assistant should work on the computer you already have.

Most AI assistants need powerful new hardware or a constant connection to the cloud. Giving older machines a useful new job keeps them out of landfills, which makes Allura part of an e-waste mission as well as a privacy one.

ACE · her brain

The Allura Cognitive Engine.

ACE figures out what you're asking, gathers the right information, and decides how to answer.

Local models · her voice

Small models, running on your PC.

Small local language models such as SmolLM2 turn ACE's work into natural conversation. You choose which model she speaks with.

The desktop app

Designed, not just built.

A custom experience with a dark violet look, a frameless window, and a sidebar that grows as you use it.

Allura Link

Your Allura, from your phone.

Reach your own Allura from your phone through a web app, while everything still runs on your PC.

What she can do today

Useful now, and honest about it.

Research with sources

Answers questions using free, open sources, with clickable links so you can see where each answer came from.

Everyday tools

Weather, time and math, right in the conversation.

Places nearby

Finds places near you, including menus and reviews.

English and Spanish

Translates offline, and replies in the language you write in.

More than one person

Separate accounts keep each person's memory and chats private.

Looks the way you like

Several themes and small animations throughout the app.

Talks honestly

Safety comes first. She answers yes-or-no questions directly and is built to be truthful rather than just agree with you.

What we're working on

Where she's headed.

Plans, not promises. They'll land as they're ready.

  1. Stronger researchClearer, more accurate answers on follow-up and deeper questions.
  2. An honesty upgradePushing back with evidence instead of mirroring you, and staying balanced on hard topics.
  3. Visual intelligenceUnderstanding photos, like recognizing a building or where a picture was taken.
  4. Pop-up windowsCode and small diagrams right in the chat.
  5. Getting things doneRestaurant reservations and food ordering, first with your help, then on her own.
  6. Version 0.3An early alpha anyone can install on their own PC, with a built-in updater.
