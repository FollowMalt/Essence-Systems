ALLURA

A private AI that lives on your own computer.

She runs on hardware you already own, even older and low-end PCs. Your conversations, memories and settings stay on your machine. She only goes online to look things up for you.

Developer Beta · in active development

{% include screen.html app="allura" ui="chat" label="A conversation with Allura" %}

How she thinks

She thinks with *ACE,* speaks with a *small* *local* *model,* and checks every answer against the evidence before you see it.

ACE · the Allura Cognitive Engine

The thinking.

ACE understands each message, decides what work it needs, runs research and tools, checks answers against the evidence and picks what the language model sees.

+
SmolLM2 · the voice

The talking.

A small language model running on your PC writes the replies, so she sounds natural without needing a powerful new computer or the cloud.

Conversation

Talks like a friend. Answers like an expert.

Ask the way you'd text. She follows the thread, so "that one" and "the second one" mean what you meant, and yes-or-no questions get a yes or no first.

  • Remembers the topic across the conversation
  • Asks one short question when something's unclear
  • Replies in English or Spanish, whichever you write in
{% include screen.html app="allura" ui="chat" label="Allura chat" %}

Research

She shows her work. Every time.

For bigger questions, Allura searches open sources, reads the best pages and sorts every claim into facts, reports and opinions. When sources disagree, she says so and tells you which side the better sources favor.

  • Clickable sources on every answer
  • Social media chatter kept separate, never treated as proof
  • Honest about where she looked, and where she couldn't

Allura Link

Your Allura, in your pocket.

Open Allura Link on your phone and talk to the Allura on your own PC, over your private network. If the app is closed, she starts up on her own and shows you each step.

  • New phones need your approval on the PC first
  • Watch her answer appear as it's checked
  • The same pages and look as the desktop app

Private by design

Hers to run. Yours to keep.

Runs on your PC

The language model, ACE and her memory all live on your computer.

An account for everyone

Each person gets their own chats, memories and settings, kept apart.

Memory you can see

A Memory page shows what she's learned, and Learning shows what she's saved from research.

Safety first

When something sounds serious, she checks in kindly and points to real help.

Honest

No made-up facts, no fake activity, and no pretending she looked somewhere she didn't.

What we're working on

Where she's headed.

Plans, not promises. They'll land as they're ready.
