For music you actually own

Your own streaming app, iTunes and Walkman manager in one.

Astra brings back owning your music without giving up what makes streaming apps great. Import your MP3s, FLACs and CDs into one clean library; Astra fixes messy tags, finds album covers and lines your songs up with their official albums. Then play them on your computer, stream them to your phone, and keep your Walkman or iPod synced on its own.

And there's more

Everything a streaming app does, for your own collection.

Playlists that make themselves

Make and reorder your own, or let Astra Playlists build top songs, recently added, rediscover and artist mixes.

Bring your playlists

Import from Spotify and Apple Music. Songs you don't have yet drop in automatically once you add them.

Astra Rewind

Your month or year in music, built from what you actually played.

Rip your CDs

Rip to MP3 or FLAC with the album looked up for you.

Made to feel yours

Light and dark themes with five color styles, and artist pages with photos.

Always ready

Runs quietly in the tray and can start with Windows, so the phone app and syncing are always there.

Your music stays yours

Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • No account, no subscription, no ads. Astra runs on your computer.
  • Your files are never changed. Fixes, lyrics, covers and play history live in Astra's own folder.
  • Only your devices can connect. The phone app answers only devices on your private network, and needs an access code.
  • Lookups are small and optional. Album info, artist photos and lyrics are found by artist and song name only. What you listen to never leaves the computer.
  • Astra never downloads music. It works with music you already own.

What you need

A Windows 10 or 11 PC.

Optional: ffmpeg for converting, leveling and CD rips; Tailscale on your computer and phone for the phone app; a CD/DVD drive for ripping.
