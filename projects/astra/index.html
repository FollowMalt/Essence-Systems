For music you actually own

Your own streaming app, iTunes and Walkman manager in one.

Import your MP3s, FLACs and CDs into one clean library. Astra fixes messy tags, finds album covers and lines your songs up with their official albums. Then play them on your computer, stream them to your phone or TV, and keep your Walkman or iPod synced on its own.

Living themes · Astra 1.2

Astra, alive.

The background moves, and it takes on the colors of the song that's playing. When the song changes, the colors fade into the new ones. Themes restyle the whole app too: the cards, headings and corners. Pick one below.

{% include screen.html ui="home" theme="cosmos" label="Astra with a living theme" %}

Cosmos. Deep black space with real-looking stars that twinkle, a faint Milky Way tinted by the song, and the odd shooting star.

Classic. The familiar Astra look, with softer, rounder cards.

Retro, 1985. A striped sunset going down into the ocean, palms swaying, and an Art Deco hotel in pink and teal neon with a flickering "ASTRA" sign. Cards get pink neon edges and teal italic headings.

Futuristic. A city at night in the rain, with neon signs that buzz and drop out, flying cars, searchlights and a hologram billboard. Cards get sharp corners, a cyan glow and monospace headings.

Koi pond. You're looking down into a pond. The koi cruise, rest, dart off and come up to kiss the surface, light dances on the bottom, and petals drift on the water. It rains on the pond when it's raining where you are. Cards look like frosted glass.

Outside. A field in the real weather where you are, with wind, clouds, rain, snow, fog, storms, sunrise and sunset, and the moon and stars at night. The weather lands on Astra itself: snow piles up on the cards, rain drips off their edges and leaves rest on them. "Try any weather" lets you preview it all.

  • Easy on the battery. Effects can be set to Full, Balanced or Still. Nothing moves while Astra is minimized, and Battery saver keeps it still on the phone.
  • Light or dark. Classic, Koi pond and Outside work in light mode, which has been reworked. Cosmos, Retro and Futuristic stay dark. You can also turn themes off for Astra's plain look.
  • Private weather. Outside only uses the town you type in Settings, never GPS. It checks open-meteo.com at most every 30 minutes and remembers the last answer for when you're offline.

Songs & albums

The whole collection, beautifully kept.

Browse by song or album, jump with the A–Z index, and search everything at once. Every album card picks up the colors of its cover.

  • Best-quality copy kept, duplicates skipped
  • Covers found for you
  • Your original files are never changed

Artist pages

Their whole story, in one place.

Each artist page opens with a big header in the artist's colors, then the full discography from your library: albums, EPs, singles, compilations, deluxe editions and soundtracks. Below that, every song you have and the artists they've worked with.

  • Play or shuffle the whole artist
  • Collaborators link to their own pages
  • Artist photos looked up by name only
{% include screen.html ui="artist" label="Astra artist page" %}

Playlists

Make your own. Or let Astra.

Build and reorder your own playlists, or let Astra Playlists put together your top songs, recently added, rediscoveries and artist mixes, updated as you listen. New in 1.2: give any playlist its own picture, from a photo or the camera, and its own color.

  • Import from Spotify and Apple Music, or .txt and .m3u files on the phone
  • Songs matched even when they're named a bit differently, with a preview first
  • Sync any playlist to your players
{% include screen.html ui="playlists" label="Astra playlists" %}

Listening

A player that glows with the song.

Now Playing fills the screen with the colors of whatever's on, with a studio-style level meter under the cover. Click it and the new EQ opens right over the artwork. Turn to Up Next, or to timed lyrics that follow along line by line.

  • A 10-band EQ with presets
  • One tidy row of buttons; each one cycles through its choices
  • Lyrics running early or late? Nudge them sooner or later
{% include screen.html ui="eq" label="Astra Now Playing with the EQ open" %}

Astra Radio · New in 1.1

Your playlists, on the air.

Every playlist and every Astra mix is a station on the FM dial, playing all day in that day's shuffle. Tune in and you join mid-song, right where the station is. Skip and you change station.

  • Your computer, phone and TV hear the same song at the same second
  • On the phone, a little static between stations
  • Stations keep their spot on the dial
{% include screen.html ui="radio" label="Astra Radio" %}{% include screen.html device="phone" ui="phone-radio" label="Astra Radio on the phone" %}

Smart Library

It tidies up. You stay in charge.

Smart Library finds missing details, wrong albums, missing covers and duplicates, and shows the evidence for every suggestion. Nothing changes until you press Apply, and everything can be undone.

  • Checks songs against online catalogs
  • Merges duplicates and tidies albums
  • Full history with undo
{% include screen.html ui="smart" label="Astra Smart Library" %}

Walkman & iPod

Plug it in. It just syncs.

Sony Walkmans, including Japanese models, and iPod shuffles get your playlists in formats they can play. In 1.1 every player gets a compact card with how full it is and the things you do most. Everything else lives on that player's own page: previews, backups, playlists, audio quality and wallpapers.

  • Auto-sync when a player is plugged in
  • Ten original wallpapers, or your own photo
  • Astra only ever removes what it put there
