What Astra is

A music app that feels like a quiet place.

Astra brings your music library together and lets you manage the devices you listen on, inside an interface inspired by koi ponds, plants and water.

01 · Library

Your whole library, arranged.

Browse and manage your music collection in one place, with glass panels that keep the focus on what you're listening to.

02 · Devices

Every device, in one view.

See and manage the devices connected to Astra without leaving the app.

03 · Interface

Designed to be calm.

Translucent glass, natural environments and soft reflections. Astra is meant to be somewhere you enjoy spending time.
