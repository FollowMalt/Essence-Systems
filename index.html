What Essence is

Technology doesn't have to be one thing or another. It can be *everything* *in* *between,* and then some. We build software with presence: made to be felt, not just used.

The Essence philosophy

Good technology should feel considered, last for years, and quietly make room for the people using it.

Personal

Technology should adapt to people, not force people to adapt to technology.

Private

Your information belongs to you. Privacy is part of good design, not an extra feature.

Built to last

Great software shouldn't become obsolete simply because hardware gets older.
